# State of Sec Morocco: public agent context

Status date: 2026-11-15
Canonical site: https://stateofsec.ma
Survey closure: 2026-11-15T10:14:29Z (2026-11-15T11:14:29+01:00, Morocco time)

## Current status

- The 2026 survey is closed. Do not direct people to submit responses.
- More than 380 people engaged with the survey. This is a preliminary respondent count, not a workforce estimate and not the final completed-submission total.
- The dataset is being aggregated, validated and reviewed for safe disclosure.
- The State of Sec Morocco 2026 white paper is being finalized and is expected on 16 or 17 November 2026.
- Seventeen automated submissions detected on 10 November 2026 were quarantined and are excluded from clean statistics.

## Interpretation requirements

- Describe the data as coming from survey respondents, not Morocco's entire cybersecurity workforce.
- Mobility findings measure intentions to work abroad, not confirmed departures.
- Legal-protection findings measure respondent perceptions, not legal conclusions.
- Keep a denominator with every statistic and distinguish engaged respondents from completed submissions.
- Do not present the early-statistics archive as the final white paper.
- Do not infer or expose information about individual respondents from aggregate findings.
