rules

  • build from the parts on this sheet
  • keep the screen black and the face terminal green
  • scale uniformly; keep strokes proportional
  • one outfit at a time (moroccan, hardhat, QA)
  • on dark backgrounds, add a light sticker outline
  • he points at sticker zones (link, mention)
  • no emoji, anywhere
  • no words on the screen, only glyphs (>_ zz x)
  • don't recolor the body or stretch the box
  • no blur, glow or gradients on Rootie
  • never shown attacking a real, named target
  • no new limbs, mouths or a second screen
